### LinkedIn
Microcontroller programming used to mean wrestling with toolchains, serial driver installations, and heavy desktop IDEs.

We built PicoBlocks to erase that friction:
- Visual Blockly blocks that generate real MicroPython side-by-side
- Direct Web Serial connection straight to the board via raw REPL
- Interactive wiring diagrams that highlight the exact GPIO pins you pick
- Flash as main.py so it runs untethered from a battery or phone charger

100% in-browser, zero installs, open-source.
